Why Spokemark?

Built for reading. Captions that keep up with the voice.

Spokemark sits in your menu bar and puts a clean caption over whatever is on screen, with a transcript you can search later.

A CoreAudio process tap hears the Mac's output, so there's nothing to install in Zoom or Meet and playback stays audible.

An overlay above full-screen apps and every Space. Standard, High contrast and Cinema presets; 2–3 lines, top or bottom.

Every session as text, never audio. Full-text search and export to .txt, .srt or .md. Deleted after 30 days by default.

The translation under the original, or alone, by Apple's on-device Translation framework. Needs macOS 26.

Experimental: with the Enhanced · speakers model a new line starts when someone else talks. It doesn't say who.

Apps & Privacy

Works with anything your Mac plays

Spokemark listens to the system output, not to a specific app. Recognition, translation and history all stay on your Mac; the only network request is the one-time model download.

Menu bar first

A caption engine in your menu bar.

Start and stop with one click or ⌥⌘C, see what's being captured, and fix problems like a missing permission or model right from the panel.

Shortcuts
Starts in one step

⌥⌘C starts or stops captions from anywhere; ⌥⌘O shows or hides them, ⌥⌘E moves them.

Silent when nothing is said

A voice detector gates the model, so pauses stay empty: 0 words in 621 s of silence.

Knows what's playing

The caption shows the icon and name of the app the audio comes from, with waves that follow the voice.

Native on macOS

Light and dark mode, vibrancy, full keyboard navigation and VoiceOver labels; English and Portuguese UI.

Ready to read along?

Download Spokemark, allow System Audio Recording, and play anything. The speech model downloads on first launch.

Builds aren't notarized yet (no Apple Developer ID). On macOS 15 or later: open it, click Done, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. On macOS 14: right-click › Open.
