# Landing page v3

Branch `landing-v3`. The visual language follows a Dribbble shot chosen by the project owner: "Sonivale — AI Audio SaaS Landing Page" by Bayu Aji Sadewa for Korsa. It was analysed in the owner's browser on 2026-10-12. Its images aren't stored in the repository.

## What was adapted, and what wasn't

| From the shot (language) | Spokemark version |
|---|---|
| Framed white sheet on a light grey page | Same structure: `.sheet` on `#ECEDF1` |
| Giant wordmark, tagline and CTA row above it | "SPOKEMARK" at up to 260 px; the tagline says what it is and the CTAs sit on the right |
| Product card floating over the wordmark, with small stat cards | An HTML replica of the real menu bar panel, replaying the real `jfk.wav` log; the two stats come from `RESULTS.md` |
| Line-wave bundle: grey on the left, accent on the right | Canvas lines: irregular "raw audio" on the left that settle into an ordered blue bundle on the right. The energy follows the demo's speech and pause steps. |
| Testimonial block, avatars | **Not used** (no testimonials exist). Replaced by a one-sentence statement of what the app does. |
| "By the numbers" (12M+, 85k…) | Measured numbers only: 0.45 s, 2.3 %, 0, 179 ms |
| Three feature cards with mini UIs | Overlay, search and translation mini UIs, with real text |
| Glow statement with floating quotes | Floating cards hold the run's **real hypotheses** (crossed out where Whisper corrected itself), not quotes |
| About paragraph and CTA | The privacy statement, three facts and the `lsof` check |
| Numbered accordion "Features (05)" | Six real features, with light screenshots |
| Icon grid with one highlighted tile | Kinds of sound sources, the app icon highlighted, and the measured pipeline as chips |
| Dark closing band, use-case strip, giant lowercase wordmark, boxed footer nav | Same structure, with requirements, first launch and build from source added |

Not copied: Sonivale's text, its violet palette (replaced by the brand electric blue `#1658FA` and periwinkle `#8EA9FF`), the vinyl illustration (replaced by the app icon), and its images or code.

**Type:** DM Sans (OFL, opsz + wght 300–600, a 42 KB subset). It's the shot's typeface class: a geometric grotesk with good medium weights.

## Verification (2026-10-13)

- **Screenshots:** `screenshots/v3/final-{390,768,1440,1920}.webp`. No horizontal scroll at any width.
- **Lighthouse mobile** (Pages-like local server, 2 runs): Performance 100, Accessibility 100, Best Practices 100, SEO 100; LCP 1.5 s, CLS 0, TBT 0 ms.
- **Animation:** 60 fps. It pauses outside the viewport and in hidden tabs. With reduced motion it shows one still frame and the full transcript.
- **Accessibility:** one `h1` (the visible wordmark plus a full sentence for screen readers), every image has `alt`, all old anchors resolve, keyboard order is logical, and there's a Pause control.
- **Links:** every external link returns 200.
- **Project:** typecheck and the 46 unit tests pass.
- **Removed:** the v2 WebGL ribbon (`js/ribbon.js`, `dev/ribbon/`) and the v2 dark images and Inter font.
